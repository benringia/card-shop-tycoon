# Pet Moves Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Finish four Pet button moves (Healing Rain, Vine Snare, Overcharge, Curse) with their own looks, sounds and motion, each a clear step up from grade C to S.

**Naming:** client code requires the config as `MoveFxConfig` (`Config/MoveFx`) and the looks module as `MoveFx` (`Util/MoveFx`); below, `MoveFxConfig.X` is the config.

**Architecture:** The sim (`server/Util/BattleSim`) gains Healing Rain's heal ticks and Overcharge's orb count, sent to clients as events. The looks live in a new client module `Util/MoveFx`, driven by `Util/FightFx` from those events, with every per-grade number in a new plain-data `Config/MoveFx` and the pure choices in `Util/MoveFxRules` (Lune-tested). A Studio-only preview plays any move at any grade on your summoned creature against a dummy, through the real `FightFx` path.

**Tech Stack:** Roblox Luau (Rojo), Lune tests (`lune run tests/run`), Python + Pillow for textures, Roblox Studio MCP for checks and image uploads.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-pet-moves-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- The owner's rule: "the higher the creature grade the better the skill must look and feel whether its animation or visual effects or both." Every grade a clear step up; never a copy.
- Combat UI untouched, apart from the Tide Pet button tooltip: "Heal your creature over time."
- Healing Rain: kind `Rain`, name "Healing Rain", 25% of max HP over 5 ticks of 5%, one a second, the first a second after it lands; recast restarts, never stacks; faint or swap-out stops it; only the caster's owner's own creatures.
- Vine Snare 40% slower for 4 s, Overcharge 3 attacks twice as fast, Curse 30% weaker for 5 s: numbers unchanged.
- Grade changes only look, sound and motion, never numbers.
- Sounds: Roblox-licensed only (Pro Sound Effects or APM via the toolbox API, memory `licensed-sound-search`); never user uploads.
- Curse circle: a seven-point star in a ring, not a five-point pentagram.
- Low graphics (the `Util/Extras` switch off): a move plays no richer than its B look.
- 60 fps on phones in a group boss fight with four creatures casting; effects inside `AttackFx.MAX_ACTIVE` / `MAX_ACTIVE_LOW` and cleaned up.
- Server authoritative: heal ticks happen in the sim; orbs follow sim events.
- Project rules (CLAUDE.md): numbers in `src/shared/Config`, pure rules in Roblox-free modules, `--!strict`, scripts only in the repo, every remote in `src/shared/Remotes.luau`, SourceOrder test (no local used above its definition).

## Review Focus

1. A Rain caster's creature swapped out or fainting mid-heal: the ticks stop (Task 1 test).
2. Rain cast again while it runs: 5 fresh ticks, never 10 (Task 1 test).
3. Group boss fight: Rain's ticks never heal another player's creature or the player (Task 1 test).
4. An Overcharged creature stunned through a fast attack: that orb fizzles with an event, the next normal attack spends nothing (Task 2 test).
5. A creature with no grade (a monster's view, an old copy) or the low-graphics switch off: C look, or capped at B (Task 3 test).
