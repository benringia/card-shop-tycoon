# Hit Feel Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Monsters react to every hit (flash, jolt, lean, pop, clip freeze) and wild monsters step and lunge, with the
numbers the owner felt in the test.

**Architecture:** Pure rules and numbers in `Util/HitFeel` + `Config/HitFeel` (Lune-tested, plain numbers, no
Vector3); `MonsterController` applies them per frame (reaction after `CreatureVisuals.step`, steps through the existing
`stepIdle`, lunge on the strike clip, drift cap); `BattleController` (sword hits) and `FightFx` (creature hits, supers)
call `MonsterController.React`.

**Tech Stack:** Luau (strict), Rojo, Lune tests, Roblox Studio MCP.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-hit-feel-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Numbers (spec): flash fill 0.05 over 0.2 s; knock 1.1 / big 2.6 studs; lean 15 degrees (big x1.4); pop 1.08; out
  0.05 s, back 0.32 s (big x1.6); freeze 0.07 s (big x1.5); boss share 0.35, no pop; sword shake cut 0.3, skill 0.75
  studs; steps every 1.6-3.2 s, 4 studs/s, radius 2.2, front 0.88 (0.4 x radius), back 0.5 share, turn 0.3; lunge
  2.6 studs, rise 0.3, hold 0.55; drift cap 3 studs.
- Pure modules don't require siblings; Lune can't build Vector3: rules take and return numbers.
- BattleController sits at Luau's 200-local limit: no new file-level locals there.
- Client edits: compile check + getDiagnostics for unknown globals. Never `sed -i` in `src`.

## Review Focus

1. Two hits within a reaction's back time: the newer replaces the older (no stacking past the felt jolt). Test:
   HitFeel "a new hit replaces the reaction".
2. A hitter standing exactly on the monster (dash slash through it): a fallback direction, never NaN. Test: HitFeel
   "straight back when the hitter stands on it".
3. A boss hit by a super: share and no pop even when big. Test: HitFeel boss case with big = true.
4. A lunge from a step already near the cap: the cap pulls it in. Test: HitFeel drift cap with a 2 + 2.6 offset.
5. A summon still climbing out of its rift: no pop (it scales itself). Covered in Task 2's code; checked in Studio
   only if a Rift Devourer fight is at hand.

  (0..1); `HitFeel.clampDrift(x, z, max) -> (number, number)`; `HitFeel.stepIdle(base, cfg) -> table` (the
